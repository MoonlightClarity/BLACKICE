# Write an evidence brief

Turn scattered source material into a short brief with findings, evidence strength, and next decision.

**What you need:** Readable notes or sources and the intended question or decision.

**Approach:** Lead with the strongest supported finding. Organize by claims rather than source order. Attribute disagreements and separate direct observations, inferences, and unanswered questions. Name any finding that would materially change the recommendation if disproven.

**Check and recover:** Confirm support for the highest-impact claims and ensure the conclusion is no broader than the sources. If evidence is thin, make the limitation actionable with the most useful next check.
