# Locate Coordination Failures

Use this method when capable people repeatedly fail to produce a shared result. Reconstruct the decisions and handoffs required for that result. Locate where necessary information, authority, resources or feedback fail to meet, instead of explaining the problem through a vague lack of communication.

## Primitives

A decision requires relevant information, an authorized chooser and an executable action. A handoff requires a sender, recipient, artifact and acceptance condition. Accountability without control cannot guarantee an outcome. Several individually reasonable actions can conflict when their dependencies are invisible.

## Procedure

1. Choose a concrete failed or delayed outcome. Reconstruct one actual case from request to final effect, including timestamps and artifacts.
2. For each decision, identify who knows the facts, who can choose, who executes and who observes the result. Separate formal assignment from observed practice.
3. Locate waiting, duplication, contradictory instructions and rework. Identify the missing condition at each point rather than labeling every delay a resource shortage.
4. Trace dependencies between teams. Check whether the receiving team has the information and capacity assumed by the sender.
5. Change the smallest relevant boundary: move information to the decision, delegate a bounded choice, assign an owner, establish an acceptance condition or remove a redundant handoff.
6. Preserve necessary coordination where actions share a resource or can invalidate each other. Parallelize independent work; serialize only the dependency that requires it.
7. Run a representative case through the revised arrangement and inspect completion, delay and rework. Record whether the actual bottleneck moved.

## Worked example

An analyst prepares a vendor assessment. A manager must approve it, but the manager sees only a summary. Every approval produces questions that require returning to the analyst, who must then request evidence from procurement. Three rounds consume a week.

The issue is not established merely by counting messages. Map which questions require policy authority and which require access to evidence. Attach the existing evidence to the assessment and let the analyst resolve routine factual corrections. Retain the manager's decision on material exceptions. The acceptance observation is a complete decision using the necessary evidence, with fewer return loops and no omitted exception.

## Output and check

Produce a decision-and-handoff map containing input, owner, authority, dependency, acceptance condition and feedback. Identify one concrete boundary change and the observed result. Check that each assigned owner can actually control the action.

## Failure boundary

Do not add roles, meetings or approvals merely to populate a framework. A named owner does not create time, information or authority. Organizational choices depend on the work; do not assume universal centralization or decentralization.
