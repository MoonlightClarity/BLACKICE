# BLACKICE 2.39.35 - Keep scheduled work moving through discovery

- Recognize new evidence collected during an authorized discovery task so a scheduled job can continue across model turns.
- Keep completion tied to the actual acceptance criteria and verified outcomes. Repeated observations and uncertain effects still cannot establish success.
- Preserve the prior job receipt and recorded operations if work stops before completion.
- Help Planning choose a bounded public research entry point when the task authorizes it.
- Give readiness checks and schedule activation up to three minutes in the Tasks screen.

The Windows installer updates BLACKICE while keeping its existing profile and schedules.
