# BLACKICE 2.33.8: less to hold in mind

Each workspace now opens around the current task. Secondary lists, editors, maps and settings open when you choose them. The visual hierarchy also separates the black-ice imagery from the information you need to read.

## Clearer information and black-ice imagery

- Black-ice texture appears in the surrounding space and selected headers, with quiet, neutral reading surfaces.
- Primary actions and selected views have stronger contrast. Supporting controls stay secondary.
- Text, focus indicators and status cues retain distinct roles, with warnings and recovery actions visible.

## Focused workspaces

- Chat starts with the conversation and composer. The chat list and plan panel open on demand and remember an explicit choice. Model, reasoning, planning, attachment, Send and Stop controls remain available.
- Activity puts the current result and updates before workflow and worker details. Recovery actions remain visible. Browser navigation remains beside the address field; inspection tools open separately.
- Planning shows the board, saved-plan library or editor one at a time. Switching views retains drafts and selection. Validation opens the affected field.
- Swarms puts the selected swarm, a short objective and the next task first. The library, task details and management actions open on demand.
- Schedules separates saved schedules from job history. Compact rows retain timing and status, with additional actions under Details. A visible attention cue opens the jobs that need review.
- Settings starts with everyday choices. Connector, password manager, remote access and advanced preferences use disclosures that retain their fields and state. Pending connector requests remain visible.
- Connections keeps the default model and saved connections first, with provider setup under Add a provider.

Four primary tabs remain in the header. A compact Change area chooser connects Planning, Swarms and Schedules, and all destinations remain available in the workspace menu and command palette.

The changes preserve unsent work, session schedule drafts, explicit panel choices, keyboard navigation and visible errors. Navigation itself does not submit tasks or start workers.
