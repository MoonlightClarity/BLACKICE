# BLACKICE 2.34.1

The V2 interface retains its organized workspaces while restoring BLACKICE's visual identity through a new, independent SVG ice scene, angular display typography, darker reading surfaces and clearer controls. Native dropdown menus use explicit matching foreground/background colors and respect Windows high contrast colors.

Conversation and task management again expose archiving, recoverable deletion, restoration and selection of multiple records. Planning projects retain their separate archive/restore semantics; saved workflow templates and schedules expose the removal actions their existing storage supports. Knowledge offers direct restoration from Trash while keeping revision history available.

This update preserves the real in-app Chromium browser, saved work, direct connection and explicit classic recovery route. Automated acceptance runs remain hidden. Installation and validation evidence for this local build is recorded in the continuation checkpoint; no public publication is performed by packaging or installation.
