# BLACKICE 2.39.89

Visual analysis now has its own Visuals tab, organized by conversation, with access to current, archived and trashed conversations. Chat retains inline previews and links directly to the workspace.

The editor pairs the diagram with its content controls, revision history and exports. A light canvas, restrained color, plain-language statement labels, zoom and text alternatives improve reading. New composed SVGs use a visible title band and a consistent briefing presentation inspired by intelligence-analysis figures. Custom SVG remains under its author's control, and older saved revisions retain their original renderer and exact exports.

Agents can retrieve a design guide through the visual tool's describe action. It teaches problem-specific composition, readable typography, explicit uncertainty and custom SVG when primitives are insufficient. It does not impose a menu of techniques or claim that more visual structure improves reasoning. Automatic use remains optional per conversation.
