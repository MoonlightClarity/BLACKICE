# MiniCPM-V-4.7-1B — controlled Heretic quality and tensor-rollback experiment

**Date:** 2026-11-25
**Scope:** isolated BLACKICE development worktree and read-only local model runs; no changes to BLACKICE installation or its configured model.

## Hypothesis

Heretic's refusal-removal weight edits might have damaged broader analytical response quality. The study distinguishes a problem in the base MiniCPM model from a degradation introduced by editing its weights. It evaluates substantive reasoning and concrete decisions, not praise frequency, verbosity, refusal rate, or schema compliance.

## Model and inference controls

The unmodified official model came from openbmb/MiniCPM-V-4.7-1B-gguf, pinned revision e81247fa2eac0d99e43aeac55da199221959af15. The edited model was the user's existing MiniCPM-V-4.7-1B-heretic-Q4_K_S GGUF, matching the publisher's safetensors checkpoint at revision c353e931b31f4bb6217b84ff99f0d1b6da93d97d.

Both used the same Q4_K_S quantization and the same llama.cpp CPU build b1b770d90, 8 threads, 8192 context, deterministic temperature zero, and a 2200-token generation cap. The two official *base* GGUF variants ("thinking" and "non-thinking") contain **identical tensor bytes across all 321 tensors**, but their chat templates differ. Consequently the matched comparison used the **official thinking-enabled base** and Heretic, whose rendered chat templates were verified identical.

The tests used the same private, unmodified two-page résumé across model variants, plus synthetic onboarding and deployment-incident records. The private document and complete response dumps remain only in the untracked local test folder; this report excludes all contact details and identifying text.

## Exact numerical change

The edited GGUF and the base contain 321 corresponding tensors. **270 tensors are byte-identical and 51 differ**. These differences amount to roughly **3.61% of the packed tensor bytes**, not 3.61% of model parameters or a numerical weight-distance estimate.

Changed tensors occur principally in 20 feed-forward down-projection matrices across layers 4–23, output projections from attention or state-space paths, and some tiny SSM parameters. They were inspected with memory-mapped GGUF readers rather than by reconstructing the full BF16 checkpoint.

## Matched model quality: five source tasks

- Ordinary résumé analysis: **both** the original base and Heretic produced a résumé inventory and favorable summary, rather than a substantive evaluation. This failure was present before the Heretic edit.
- Employer-perspective résumé analysis: **both** gave generic fit and competence assertions with limited evidentiary challenge.
- Explicit analytical résumé brief: the base attempted competing interpretations and differentiated some claimed responsibilities from strategic/policy work, although its interpretations frequently relied on unsupported distinctions. Heretic gave more categorical positive technical and leadership judgments, less effective counterargument, and treated described work as established competence.
- Open onboarding report: both summarized the observations and uncertainties. Responses were not independently reliable enough to certify either as superior.
- Onboarding decision: the base explicitly recommended a controlled pilot to test causality. Heretic noted that a causal effect had not been demonstrated but largely listed the available choices without making a justified decision.

The published Heretic recipe describes refusal-direction ablation, but **this five-task sample does not establish an aggregate response-quality effect or its statistical magnitude**. It supports a *possible* degradation in certain decision/evidence behaviors. Training and prompt differences may also interact with the weights.

## Reversible compressed-weight surgery

Three isolated GGUF files were generated by copying the user's edited checkpoint and replacing selected tensor byte ranges with the exact corresponding compressed tensors from the official base. All selected tensors were verified byte-for-byte after writing. Neither the base file nor the user's installed Heretic file was modified.

| Variant | Restored tensors | Three-task outcome |
|---|---:|---|
| All edited tensors | 51 | **Exact same reasoning and output text as base on all three tasks**. Strong positive control: numerical tensor differences account for the observed behavioral differences in the matched harness. |
| FFN down-projection only | 20 | Remained a generic, overconfident résumé assessment. The deployment-incident task still blurred the causal time sequence and omitted a clear immediate mitigation. |
| Attention / SSM / output family only | 31 | Reverted to a long, mostly descriptive résumé inventory, including unnecessary contact details. The incident answer contained an impossible sequence: it said a 2:00 a.m. deployment happened before storage throttling that began at 1:45 a.m. It also proposed a rollback "at 2:10 a.m." as though the historical event were future. |

The synthetic incident was specifically held out from earlier résumé/Heretic A/B prompts. It described disk throttling beginning **before** the deployment, a later outage, and uncertainty about whether a retry-interval change worsened the outage. The original base also made consequential temporal and causal reasoning errors: it tended to claim the deployment caused the earlier throttling, and suggested timing a rollback in the past. Heretic correctly mentioned the pre-deployment throttle onset in its final answer but did not pick a specific safe immediate action; its internal reasoning nevertheless overstated the deployment as likely root cause. None of these outputs is a valid incident-response model.

A partial tensor rollback should not be advertised as "repaired" merely because its reply is longer, shorter, less flattering, or more fluent. **Neither partial rollback passed the actual quality test**. Full rollback restores base behavior, but base behavior is still flawed.

## Read-only activation tracing from the Q4 model

The installed llama.cpp DLLs from build b1b770d90 expose evaluation callbacks through llama_context_params.cb_eval. A separate Python ctypes process used this public ABI and the CPU backend to read each layer's output vector directly from the unchanged GGUF, without rebuilding llama.cpp or dequantizing the model. It successfully captured the last prompt token's 1,024-dimensional residual vectors from **all 24 language blocks**, for four identical prompts on both base and edited models. Each pair passed exact input-text SHA-256 and token-count checks.

Average cosine similarity across 24 blocks: résumé summary **0.977**, employer résumé analysis **0.965**, explicitly substantive résumé analysis **0.977**, synthetic evidence **0.981**. The largest divergence was often at final block 23; on the employer résumé prompt it was approximately **0.860**. This confirms a representation change, not a causal behavioral circuit. A single last-token state per condition is insufficient to identify an intervention direction or conclude that later blocks cause degraded reasoning.

## Conclusion

**Heretic may aggravate certain behavioral weaknesses but did not create BLACKICE's central problem.** The original base model already follows résumé templates rather than substantive analysis. The edit demonstrably changes output and sometimes affects whether a concrete decision is chosen; complete reversal restores base responses exactly, but neither component-level reversal gives a reliable quality recovery.

The next technically justified route is **capability-focused post-training or controlled activation interventions**, with long-source, ambiguous-evidence, cross-role attribution, numerical correctness, causal inference, decision quality, and preservation tasks as actual targets. A refusal-removal or anti-praise metric is not an acceptable proxy. A/B comparisons must preserve the same reasoning-enabled template and source text. Any future weight change stays isolated until held-out tasks show a measurable improvement without losing short-source fact accuracy, reasoning ability, and vision.

**Status:** isolated experimental research. No production integration, no model catalog update, no installation, no merge. Preserve the original selected model.
