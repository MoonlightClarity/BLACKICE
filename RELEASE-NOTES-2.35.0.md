# BLACKICE 2.35.0

Planning opens on the selected saved plan's step map, with dependencies, branch focus, an outline alternative, and one step inspector. Project maps show reviewed progress and real milestones. Shared goal and step notes keep explicit revision-checked saves and recoverable local drafts.

Tasks opens on a status board. Schedules and job history have their own views, keeping their controls and unfinished forms available without stacking them above the work. Activity separates workflow inspection, task history, and applications. Archive, recoverable Trash, restore, search, multi-select, and bounded loading remain available.

The dark-ice artwork and graphite palette are retained. Visual navigation inspects saved state; it does not change execution or invent dates for undated steps.
