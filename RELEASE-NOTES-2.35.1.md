# BLACKICE 2.35.1

The desktop opens its local workspace independently of reconnecting the saved HTTPS route. Remote connection status continues to show its actual progress and errors. Shutdown still drains admitted setup and closes the owned route.

Saved direct-action receipts are validated with at most four concurrent readers. Startup still validates the complete index before admitting actions, preserves receipt hashes and restart recovery, and never replays interrupted actions.

Bounded startup phase timings help diagnose slow launches without recording paths, URLs, credentials, prompts or receipt contents. The visual workspaces, dark-ice artwork and saved organization states are unchanged.
