# BLACKICE 2.39.90

Visuals stay with the response. The dedicated Visuals tab and per-conversation opt-in toggle are removed; the capability remains available for selective use. Empty visual panels disappear, and a small Inspect figure control opens editing, history and exports.

The authoring guide now leads with purpose-built SVG composition and three contrasting analytical examples, replacing the generic box-flow example. It emphasizes meaningful spatial structure, restrained BLACKICE colors, readable hierarchy and avoiding decorative process diagrams. A native preview action returns the actual rendered image plus advisory clipping and text-overlap diagnostics before a draft is saved. The preview uses a disposable sandboxed renderer with no external resources or permissions.

Existing artifacts, custom colors, historical renderers and exact exports are preserved. Rendering tests establish legibility and editing behavior; comparative reasoning quality and the quality of future model-authored figures remain to be evaluated.
